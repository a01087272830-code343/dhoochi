newuploder

Owner-only upload utility for the YouTube channel “一もはや芸術” · Nov 2026

newuploder is a lightweight program that the owner of 一もはや芸術 runs on their own machine to post completed art shorts to their channel. There are no other users.

How it works

  • The owner keeps finished clips in one folder, each with a matching .txt holding title, description and hashtags.
  • Every morning newuploder uploads the next clip and sets its release time.
  • Afterwards it notes the public view, like and comment counts of the latest clips for the owner.

Boundaries

No changes to channel settings or monetization, no comments or messages, no data leaves the owner's computer except the upload to YouTube.
